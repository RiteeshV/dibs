🏷️ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026 · Applies Australia-wide

Who we are

Kerbit is a listing and coordination service that unifies Australia's household hard-rubbish, vehicle, property, jobs, and services marketplaces in one place — helping neighbours give away, sell, or dispose of unwanted items before their council collection day, alongside broader classifieds like cars, rentals, and local services.

What we collect

Email address (login only), a password (stored as a salted hash — we can never read it), your suburb and state, your council pickup weekday, the listings you post (title, description, category, price, photos/videos you choose to upload), search terms you type into Kerbit's own search bar, and activity records (claims, handoffs, ratings, flags, receipts, eco points).

Kerbit Concierge (optional council-pickup coordination)

If you opt in to "Kerbit Concierge" when posting a household item, you may provide a street address so we can help coordinate council collection. This address is kept private — it is never shown to other users, never shown on your listing, and is used only for that coordination. It is not required to use Kerbit.

Vehicles, property, jobs and services listings

These categories don't follow the council-truck fallback — they're plain listings with no collection deadline. Kerbit does not run vehicle history, PPSR, registration, roadworthiness, ownership, or background checks, and does not verify property titles, rental legality, or the identity, licensing, or qualifications of anyone posting a job or service. That verification is on you — see the Terms for details.

Kerbit's smart search

The search bar on the feed interprets what you type (keywords, a category like "car", a price like "under $500") entirely on your own device to filter and rank listings already loaded in the app. It is not sent to any third party and does not use AI models or external services — that's a separate, clearly labelled "eBay AU" button (see below).

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google," Google shares your email and name with us so we can create or match your account. We verify Google's token directly — we never see or store your Google password.

What other users see

Only an automatically generated anonymous handle (e.g. Kerb-Wombat-482), your suburb, your posted items, and your eco points (shown on a suburb leaderboard). Your email, name, and any real identity are never shown to other users. Kerbit never asks for your phone number. Handles are assigned by the system and can be regenerated at any time; users can never choose their own handle.

Searching eBay from inside Kerbit

When you tap "eBay AU" to search elsewhere, the text you typed is sent to eBay's public Browse API to fetch results shown inside the app. No account information is sent — only the search text.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, do not show advertising, do not track your location without your action (the "Locate" button only runs when you tap it), and do not share your details with cross-posting platforms — cross-posted listings identify you only by your anonymous handle and a link back to Kerbit.

Payments and receipts

When a priced item's handoff is confirmed, Kerbit generates an online receipt identifying both parties by their anonymous handles only. If card payments are enabled, they are processed by Stripe — Kerbit never sees or stores card numbers.

Data storage and deletion

Data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. You can delete your posted items at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Moderation

Items flagged by users as irrelevant, unsafe, or suspected scams are automatically moved to observation and hidden from the public feed pending review. Flag records are kept for safety purposes.

🏷️ Kerbit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026 · Applies Australia-wide

Who we are

Kerbit is a listing and coordination service that helps Australians give away, sell, or dispose of unwanted household items before their council hard-rubbish collection day.

What we collect

Email address (login only), a password (stored as a salted hash — we can never read it), your suburb, your council pickup weekday, the items you post (title, description, photos/videos you choose to upload), and activity records (claims, handoffs, ratings, flags, receipts).

What other users see

Only an automatically generated anonymous handle (e.g. Kerb-Wombat-482), your suburb, and your posted items. Your email, name, and any real identity are never shown to other users. Kerbit never asks for your phone number. Handles are assigned by the system and can be regenerated at any time; users can never choose their own handle.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, do not show advertising, do not track your location, and do not share your details with the cross-posting platforms — cross-posted listings identify you only by your anonymous handle and a link back to Kerbit.

Payments and receipts

When a priced item's handoff is confirmed, Kerbit generates an online receipt identifying both parties by their anonymous handles only. If card payments are enabled, they are processed by Stripe — Kerbit never sees or stores card numbers.

Data storage and deletion

Data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. You can delete your posted items at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Moderation

Items flagged by users as irrelevant, unsafe, or suspected scams are automatically moved to observation and hidden from the public feed pending review. Flag records are kept for safety purposes.

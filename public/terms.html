1. What Kerbit is

Kerbit is a listing and coordination service only, unifying household hard-rubbish giveaways with broader Australian classifieds — vehicles, property, jobs, and services — in one app. Kerbit is not a party to any exchange, does not own, inspect, transport, verify, or guarantee any item, vehicle, property, job, or service listed, and is not responsible for its condition, legality, safety, or fitness for purpose.

2. Your account

You must provide a valid email and keep your password secure, or sign in with Google. You appear to others only under a system-generated anonymous handle. One account per person. You must be at least 16 to use Kerbit.

3. Posting rules

Post only items, vehicles, property, jobs, or services you own, are authorised to list, and may lawfully give away, sell, rent, or advertise. No weapons, hazardous materials, recalled products, animals, illegal goods, or anything unsafe. Photos/videos must show the actual item or property. Listings flagged by the community are moved to observation and may be removed.

3a. Vehicles

Sellers must have the legal right to sell the vehicle and must disclose known defects, accident history, and any money owing on it. Kerbit strongly recommends buyers run an independent PPSR (Personal Property Securities Register) check and an inspection before paying. Kerbit does not verify registration, roadworthiness, odometer accuracy, or ownership, and is not a party to any vehicle sale.

3b. Property, jobs and services

Kerbit is not a licensed real estate agency, employment agency, or trades licensing body. Posters of property listings must have the right to advertise the property; posters of jobs and services must comply with Australian workplace and consumer law. Kerbit does not verify property ownership, rental bond handling, employer legitimacy, or any trade licence, qualification, or insurance claimed by a poster — check these yourself before engaging.

4. Claims and handoffs

A claim reserves an item for 2 days. Arrange pickup through the app. Meet safely: daytime handoffs, item on the kerb where possible. Kerbit is not liable for anything that occurs during a pickup.

5. Priced items, payments and receipts

Prices are set by the poster in AUD. Kerbit generates an online receipt when a priced handoff is confirmed. Where card payments are enabled they are processed by Stripe under Stripe's terms. Disputes about an item are between buyer and seller; Kerbit may assist but is not obliged to.

6. Eco points

Eco points and leaderboard tiers are a gamified way to track your impact. They have no cash value, cannot be transferred, sold, or redeemed for money, goods, or services, and may be reset or adjusted at any time.

7. Council truck fallback

For household categories, items unclaimed by your nominated council pickup day are marked "booked for the truck". You remain responsible for complying with your council's hard-rubbish rules (what may be placed out, when, and how). Vehicles, property, jobs, and services listings have no truck deadline — they simply stay listed until claimed or removed.

8. Cross-posting and search

Cross-posting tools publish listings under your Kerbit handle. Where you connect your own platform API tokens, you authorise Kerbit to post on your behalf on that platform, subject to that platform's terms. Kerbit's own in-app search matches your search text against listing keywords, category, and price — it runs in your browser and is not sent anywhere. Searching eBay from inside Kerbit is a separate feature subject to eBay's own terms of use.

9. Acceptable use

No harassment, no attempts to identify other users, no scraping, no false flags, no fraudulent listings. Breach may result in observation, suspension, or removal of your account.

To the maximum extent permitted by the Australian Consumer Law, Kerbit's liability is limited to re-supplying the service. Nothing in these terms excludes rights that cannot be excluded under Australian law.

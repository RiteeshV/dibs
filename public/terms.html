Last updated: 16 July 2026 · Applies Australia-wide

1. What Kerbit is

Kerbit is a listing and coordination service only. We help neighbours find items a new home before council pickup day. Kerbit is not a party to any exchange, does not own, inspect, transport, or guarantee any item, and is not responsible for item condition, safety, or fitness for purpose.

2. Your account

You must provide a valid email and keep your password secure. You appear to others only under a system-generated anonymous handle. One account per person. You must be at least 16 to use Kerbit.

3. Posting rules

Post only household items you own and may lawfully give away or sell. No weapons, hazardous materials, recalled products, animals, illegal goods, or anything unsafe. Photos/videos must show the actual item. Listings flagged by the community are moved to observation and may be removed.

4. Claims and handoffs

A claim reserves an item for 2 days. Arrange pickup through the app. Meet safely: daytime handoffs, item on the kerb where possible. Kerbit is not liable for anything that occurs during a pickup.

5. Priced items, payments and receipts

Prices are set by the poster in AUD. Kerbit generates an online receipt when a priced handoff is confirmed. Where card payments are enabled they are processed by Stripe under Stripe's terms. Disputes about an item are between buyer and seller; Kerbit may assist but is not obliged to.

6. Council truck fallback

Items unclaimed by your nominated council pickup day are marked "booked for the truck". You remain responsible for complying with your council's hard-rubbish rules (what may be placed out, when, and how).

7. Cross-posting

Cross-posting tools publish listings under your Kerbit handle. Where you connect your own platform API tokens, you authorise Kerbit to post on your behalf on that platform, subject to that platform's terms.

8. Acceptable use

No harassment, no attempts to identify other users, no scraping, no false flags, no fraudulent listings. Breach may result in observation, suspension, or removal of your account.

9. Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by the Australian Consumer Law, Kerbit's liability is limited to re-supplying the service. Nothing in these terms excludes rights that cannot be excluded under Australian law.
